The leftmost column is the most significant three trits of the tryte.
The topmost row is the two least significant trits of the tryte.
E.g., the character J is encoded as tryte 010T0.

Fun details:

- For all uppercase letters, swapping the sign from positive to negative makes
  them lowercase. In the same way, swapping the sign to positive for a
  lowercase letter makes it uppercase.
- Subtract 00100 (decimal 27) from a decimal digit to get its value as a tryte.
  Add the same value to convert a tryte in the correct range to its digit
  character. This also works for base 12 if you use A and B as the digits for
  10 and 11.
- Sorting strings alphabetically might be a bit of a nightmare in this system.
- Opening brackets are all positive tryte values. Swap their sign to negative
  and you get the closing versions.
